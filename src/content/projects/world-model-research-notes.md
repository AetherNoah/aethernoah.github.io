A growing knowledge base for reading and understanding world-model research. The notes are written in Chinese, with complete paper explanations, comparisons across model families, and links to original sources.

## Reading and connecting ideas

- **Model-based reinforcement learning:** Dreamer V1/V2/V3, RSSM, and latent imagination.
- **Generative and interactive world models:** Genie and the LingBot family, including latent actions and action conditioning.
- **Research topics:** action controllability, temporal credit assignment, and reinforcement learning for diffusion models.
- **Code reading:** EnerVerse-AC repository notes and source walkthroughs.
- **Embodied policies:** differentiable dynamics, unified world-action modeling, and video-based policy pretraining.
- **Agentic control and adaptation:** comparisons of native robot policies, policy harnesses, in-context demonstrations, and test-time training.

The repository also includes research questions, experiment plans, and scripts for building its paper index and checking content consistency.
